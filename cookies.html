Cookie Policy

Last updated: June 15, 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how AsheFlow uses cookies and similar tracking technologies when you visit our website or use our platform, and the choices available to you. It should be read alongside our Privacy Policy.

What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They help the site remember information about your visit, such as your login state and preferences, which can make your next visit easier and the service more useful to you. Similar technologies include local storage and pixels, which we may also use for the purposes described below.

How We Use Cookies

We use cookies for a few different purposes, grouped into the following categories:

  • Essential cookies: required for the platform to function, including keeping you signed in, securing your session, and remembering items in progress (like an unfinished onboarding step). The Service cannot operate without these.
  • Functional cookies: remember your preferences, such as theme settings or dashboard layout, so you don't have to reconfigure them on every visit.
  • Analytics cookies: help us understand how visitors use AsheFlow — which pages are viewed, which features are used most — so we can improve the product. This data is aggregated and does not identify you individually.
  • Marketing cookies: used on our public marketing pages to measure the effectiveness of campaigns and, where applicable, to show relevant AsheFlow content on other platforms. We do not use marketing cookies inside the logged-in dashboard.

Third-Party Cookies

Some cookies are set by third-party services we rely on:

  • Stripe sets cookies during checkout and billing flows to prevent fraud and process payments securely.
  • Analytics providers may set cookies to help us measure site traffic and product usage in aggregate.

These third parties have their own privacy and cookie practices, which we encourage you to review.

Managing Your Cookie Preferences

Most browsers let you control cookies through their settings, including blocking or deleting them. Look for "Privacy" or "Cookies" settings in your browser's preferences menu. Note that blocking essential cookies may prevent parts of AsheFlow — such as staying signed in — from working correctly. You can also opt out of non-essential marketing communications at any time using the unsubscribe link in our emails.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy periodically to reflect changes in the technologies we use or for legal and regulatory reasons. Material changes will be noted by updating the "Last updated" date above.
